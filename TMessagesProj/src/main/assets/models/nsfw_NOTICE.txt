nsfw_mobilenet_v2.tflite
========================

Source: GantMan/nsfw_model - https://github.com/GantMan/nsfw_model
Release: 1.2.0 (mobilenet_v2_140_224.1.zip), SavedModel "mobilenet_v2_140_224"
         https://github.com/GantMan/nsfw_model/releases/tag/1.2.0
Modification: converted to TensorFlow Lite with float16 post-training
              quantization (TensorFlow 2.18.1). Weights/architecture unchanged.

Input : float32 [1,224,224,3], RGB, pixel values scaled to [0,1] (value / 255)
Output: float32 [1,5] softmax probabilities, class order:
        0 drawings, 1 hentai, 2 neutral, 3 porn, 4 sexy
