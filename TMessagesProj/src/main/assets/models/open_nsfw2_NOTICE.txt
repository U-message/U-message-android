open_nsfw2.tflite
=================

Source: bhky/opennsfw2 (Keras port of Yahoo's Open NSFW) - https://github.com/bhky/opennsfw2
        weights "open_nsfw_weights.h5" (opennsfw2 0.x release), original model: https://github.com/yahoo/open_nsfw
Modification: an input layer was added that turns RGB 0..255 into BGR minus the Caffe mean (104, 117, 123),
              then converted to TensorFlow Lite with float16 post-training quantization (TensorFlow 2.18.1).
              Weights/architecture unchanged.

Input : float32 [1,224,224,3], RGB, 0..255 (the picture resized to 256x256, center 224x224 crop)
Output: float32 [1,2] softmax, [sfw, nsfw]
